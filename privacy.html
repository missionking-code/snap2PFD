Your files
never leave your device.

This isn't a policy written by a lawyer. It's a technical fact — and here's exactly why.

No uploads. Ever.

Every image you select is read directly by your browser using the File and Blob APIs. It is never sent to a server. There is no backend that processes your files, because there is no backend at all. The entire app is a static site.

No accounts. No tracking.

There is no sign-up, no login, no cookies for tracking, no analytics scripts.

No storage on our side.

We don't store your files — we can't, because they never reach us. The PDF you export is assembled in your browser's memory and saved directly to your device via a download. Once you close the tab, nothing remains except the file you downloaded.

Works offline after first load.

Once the page and libraries are cached by your browser, the app keeps working even with the network off. That's the practical proof that nothing you do requires a server.

The short version: your images are yours. They are processed on your device and never transmitted. If that ever changes, this page will say so first.
